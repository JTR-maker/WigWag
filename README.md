📲 Install Commo Fundamentals on your phone

This is a web app — there's no app store, no account, and no download of a file. You open one link, save it to your home screen, and from then on it launches and behaves exactly like a normal app, including working with no signal.

Open the app: https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/

⚠️ Do this once while you have internet. The first time you open the app online, it saves itself to your phone so it can run offline afterward (in the field, airplane mode, dead zones — it all still works).

🍎 iPhone / iPad

Must be done in Safari — Chrome on iPhone cannot add home-screen apps.

Open the link above in Safari.
Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bottom or top bar).
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
Tap Add (top-right).

A Commo Fundamentals icon now sits on your home screen. Open it from there and it runs full-screen with no browser bars — like a real app.

🤖 Android

Use Chrome (or any Chromium browser — Edge, Brave, Samsung Internet).

Open the link above in Chrome.
Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right).
Tap Add to Home screen (it may say Install app).
Tap Add / Install to confirm.

The icon appears in your app drawer and on your home screen, and opens full-screen.

✅ After it's installed
Works offline. Once you've opened it online a single time, it runs with no connection — reference cards, calculators, Land Nav, notes, everything.
Launches like an app. Full-screen, its own icon, no address bar.
Your notes and waypoints stay on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
To update: just open the app while you have internet once in a while — it quietly pulls the newest version in the background and uses it next launch.
🔧 Troubleshooting
Problem	Fix
"Add to Home Screen" is missing	On iPhone you must use Safari, not Chrome.
App won't load offline	Open it online one time first so it can cache itself.
Still on an old version	Open it once with internet, then fully close and reopen.
Location/compass not working	Open Land Nav → Enable sensors and allow the permission prompt.
