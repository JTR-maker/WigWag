About

Five by Five is a self-contained, offline field-comms toolkit. Everything — reference, calculators, maps, and notes — lives in a single page that runs entirely on your device once it's saved to your home screen. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Reference — a searchable library of comms fundamentals: HF/VHF propagation and NVIS, antennas & grounding, RF safety, the phonetic alphabet, prowords and readability, PACE, and a glossary you can add your own terms to.

Tools — field calculators (antenna cutting lengths, radio line-of-sight / horizon, RF power & cable loss, VSWR, TACSAT pointing, distance & bearing), coordinate conversion (lat/long ↔ MGRS / UTM, azimuth grid/magnetic/true), fill-in report builders (SALUTE, SPOT, SITREP, 9-line MEDEVAC, NBC-1), a live Zulu clock & converter, and a saved PACE plan.

Land Nav — uses your phone's own GPS and compass to show your position (grid, lat/long, altitude), walk you to a waypoint with a homing arrow and true/grid/magnetic bearings, and plot waypoints on a north-up range plot. Chain waypoints into a route and the map highlights the whole path leg by leg, with per-leg azimuth, total distance, and an ETA from your pace — plus an optional snap-to-roads overlay when you have signal.

Map — an offline-capable topographic map (USGS or OpenTopoMap) with your live position. Drop and fine-tune waypoints, and pre-download an area so it works with no signal.

Notes are private, organized in folders, and encrypted on-device behind a passphrase. Sketch Pad is a free-draw canvas with line, shape, and fill tools for quick diagrams. Hurry & Wait holds a set of offline games for the downtime.

Saved & Setup — your pinned reference cards, install instructions, encrypted backup & restore, a printable quick-reference, and theme, text-size & accessibility controls.

Unclassified, doctrine-level content only — no system-specific procedures, part numbers, configuration data, or COMSEC material. Formulas are field rules of thumb: always verify against your equipment's manual and unit SOP, and treat GPS/compass here as a backup to map & compass.

Land Nav

Offline topo map, GPS & compass, waypoints and routes.

Read first — this is a backup aid, not primary navigation. It runs only while this screen is open (no background tracking). GPS is consumer-grade (~5–20 m) and the compass drifts near metal, vehicles, and weapons — calibrate with a figure-8 and always cross-check with map & compass. Everything runs on your phone's own GPS and compass; nothing is sent anywhere.

Pace count & dead reckoning

Calibrate your pace count over a known 100 m, then get the paces to walk any leg — and project a grid from your position along a bearing.
Your pace count (paces / 100 m)
Pace count = how many of your paces cover 100 m (count every other step; typical 60–70). Projection converts your magnetic bearing to true using local declination and walks the distance from your current GPS fix.
