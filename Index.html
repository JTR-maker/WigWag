About

A general, unclassified field-radio reference and tool set: antenna and RF math, coordinate conversion, report-format builders, a live Zulu clock, and notes. Contains no system-specific operating procedures, part numbers, configuration data, or COMSEC material.

Reference formulas are field rules of thumb — always verify against your equipment's manual and your unit SOP.

🧭 Land Nav

Offline GPS + compass. Enter a grid, get walked to it.

Read first — this is a backup aid, not primary navigation. It runs only while this screen is open (no background tracking). GPS is consumer-grade (~5–20 m) and the compass drifts near metal, vehicles, and weapons — calibrate with a figure-8 and always cross-check with map & compass. Everything runs on your phone's own GPS and compass; nothing is sent anywhere.
